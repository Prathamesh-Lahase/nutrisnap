    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: November 2026

    What we collect

    NutriSnap has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or location.

    Your photos

    • Photos are resized in your browser and sent only when you tap “Analyze food”, solely to produce a nutrition estimate.
    • This site does not store your photos after analysis.

    Your diary

    Meals, goals and settings are saved only in your browser’s local storage on this device. We cannot see them. Use “Clear all data” at any time to erase everything, or clear your browser data.

    Third parties & cookies

    No advertising, trackers or analytics cookies. When you tap Analyze, your photo and meal description are sent through this site’s server to Google’s Gemini API to produce the estimate. On Google’s free tier, submitted content may be used to improve Google products, so avoid photos that show people, documents or anything private. This site does not store your photos. If you paste your own Gemini key in AI settings, it is saved only in your browser on this device and is sent only to Google with your requests.

    Your control

    You can export your diary as CSV, delete it, or decline the analysis prompt and still log foods manually.

    Children

    This tool is not intended for children under 13.

      About NutriSnap

      Smart food recognition and calorie tracking that keeps your data on your device.

      Our mission

      Make healthy eating easier to understand. Point your camera at a meal, get a quick estimate of calories, macros and ingredients, then track your day without creating an account.

      What you can do

      • Scan meals with the camera or a photo
      • Describe meals in text for better accuracy
      • Track calories, macros, water and weight
      • Review weekly reports and export CSV

      How the estimates work

      Photos are analyzed by Google’s Gemini AI model, which identifies foods, portions and likely ingredients. Results are estimates and can be off by 20% or more. They are not medical advice.

      Frequently asked questions

      Quick answers about using NutriSnap.

      How accurate are the calorie estimates?

      They are estimates, often within 15 to 25 percent. For better accuracy, enter grams, describe the meal in the text box and correct any values.

      Are my photos stored?

      No. A photo is resized on your device and sent only when you tap Analyze, to produce the estimate. This site does not keep it. It is processed by Google’s Gemini API, and on Google’s free tier submitted content may be used to improve Google products.

      Where is my diary saved?

      Only in your browser on this device. Clearing browser data or using Clear all data removes it.

      Do I need an account?

      No. There is no sign-up.

      The camera will not open. What can I do?

      Allow camera access when asked. If live camera is unavailable here, the app opens your device camera or photo picker instead.

      Can I log food without a photo?

      Yes. Use Quick add, Add custom food, or describe your meal in text.

      How is my calorie goal calculated?

      The calculator on the home page uses a standard formula based on age, sex, height, weight and activity. It is a general estimate, not medical advice.

      Can I export my data?

      Yes. Export the diary or the weekly report as CSV.
